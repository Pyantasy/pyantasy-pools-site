Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pyantasy Pools (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS app and this website. It is written for App Store transparency (Guideline 5.1) and is not a substitute for legal counsel. Contact: pyantasy+privacy@gmail.com (replace with your production address before shipping).

1. Who we are

Pyantasy Pools is a social pool-tracking app for Survivor, Pick'em, and Squares-style contests among friends. We provide software only. We do not accept, transfer, escrow, or hold entry fees or prize money.

2. Information we collect

  • Account identifiers. Sign in with Apple (Apple-provided user id and, if you share it, a relay or real email) or email address for one-time passcode sign-in.
  • Profile data you enter. Display name and optional payment handles (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal) used only to help members pay a commissioner outside the app.
  • Pool activity. Pool membership, picks, board claims, payment status marks (Paid / Unpaid / Waived), and related timestamps needed to run pools.
  • Device tokens. Apple Push Notification tokens so we can send deadline reminders and pool event alerts you opt into.
  • Notification preferences. Whether reminders are enabled and lead times you choose.
  • Advertising / analytics (when enabled). If you consent, Google AdMob and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework may use advertising identifiers and usage data to show ads and measure performance. Debug builds use Google test ad units.
  • Diagnostics. Crash, hang, and performance diagnostics delivered by Apple MetricKit (operating-system collected). We do not use a third-party crash SDK, and we do not upload crash reports to our own servers (on-device / Apple MetricKit only).

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • Operate pools: create/join, enforce pick deadlines on the server, show standings, and sync live scores.
  • Send push notifications you allow (pick reminders, results, payment updates).
  • Show ads subject to your consent choices and frequency caps.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with law.
  • Honor account deletion requests from inside the app.

4. How we share information

  • Other pool members. Display name, picks (other members’ picks only after the relevant game locks), payment status visible to commissioners as configured, and board claims on Squares.
  • Service providers. Supabase (auth, database, realtime), Apple (Sign in with Apple, push, MetricKit diagnostics), and Google (AdMob / UMP consent) process data on our behalf under their terms.
  • No sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your payment handles to process money.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

5. Payments disclaimer

Any entry fee is arranged privately between you and the pool commissioner (for example via Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal deep links). Those third-party apps have their own privacy policies. Pyantasy Pools only stores handles you choose to save and commissioner-marked payment status.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and pool data while your account is active and as needed to operate active or recently completed pools. You can delete your account from Profile in the app. Deletion removes your profile and personal identifiers we control; some aggregated or anonymized contest history may remain where required for other members’ records. Push tokens are removed when invalid or when you sign out / delete.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. Contests and fee tracking may lead to an age rating of 17+ on the App Store. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by your jurisdiction for this type of product.

8. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in the United States or other countries. By using the app you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

9. Your choices

  • System Settings → notifications and tracking for Pyantasy Pools.
  • In-app notification preferences.
  • AdMob / UMP consent forms when presented.
  • Account deletion in Profile.
  • Email us to ask questions about this policy.

10. App Store privacy nutrition label (summary)

The following maps to App Store Connect → App Privacy for the shipping iOS app (including Google AdMob / UMP and Apple Push Notification service). Exact ASC toggles must match SDKs and consent behavior before external TestFlight or App Review.

  • Contact Info. Email address (Sign in with Apple if you share it, or email OTP); name (display name). Linked to identity; not used for tracking.
  • Identifiers. User ID (account). Device / advertising identifiers when you consent to personalized ads (ATT + AdMob) — may be used for tracking per Apple’s definition.
  • Usage Data. Product interaction (pools, picks, standings). Advertising data via AdMob (impressions, performance) subject to your consent choices.
  • Diagnostics. Crash Data and Performance Data via Apple MetricKit (OS-collected). Not linked for advertising; not used for tracking. No third-party crash SDK.
  • Other data. Push notification device tokens; pool activity and optional payment handles (Venmo / Cash App / PayPal usernames only — we never process payments).

We do not sell personal information. AdMob may show non-personalized or personalized ads depending on UMP/ATT outcomes. You can reset advertising identifier and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
